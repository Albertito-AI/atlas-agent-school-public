## Critical corrections and qualifications

### Necessary and sufficient conditions

S03, Shaffer chapter 5, printed p. 47 / PDF p. 63, reverses the necessity relation in one prose account of affirming the consequent. The page's displayed argument schema is correct. For `A -> B`, **A is sufficient for B and B is necessary for A**. The module follows the explicit forms and verified truth tables. S05 section 2.7, printed pp. 85-89 / PDF pp. 92-96, supplies a cross-check. These are specific textual corrections, not a rejection of the chapter as a whole.

### Counterexample search

S05, printed p. 19 / PDF p. 26, gives an informal imagination-based validity test. The module adds a necessary qualification: failure to imagine a counterexample can reflect an incomplete search. A completed sound proof or complete decision procedure is needed for a conclusive validity result. S08 p. 19 also distinguishes an unsuccessful search from proof. The bundled propositional method enumerates every valuation within its accepted bounds.

### Truth and present evidence

S05, printed p. 150 / PDF p. 157, contains wording that associates truth with not yet being shown false. The module keeps truth separate from what has been established or refuted by available evidence. Similarly, S02 pp. 40-41 uses non-entailment in explaining why a prediction is weak. Deductive non-entailment alone does not establish weak induction; inductive support needs its own assessment.

### Sampling and explanation

S03 chapter 2 and S05 chapter 3 supply useful introductions. A larger sample can remain systematically biased; a simpler hypothesis is not automatically true; and low prior probability can be overcome by sufficiently discriminating evidence. F01 retains these qualifications and uses fully specified synthetic numbers for its calculations. It never invents missing Bayesian inputs.

### Human pedagogy and agent instruction

S09 is a historical work about human teaching. Its psychological generalisations and social assumptions are not adopted as contemporary findings about humans or agents. The module uses its methodological suggestions about comparison, testing and application as historical inspiration.

S10's "AI-native" learners are human law students. The article provides no direct test of the present school or of software agents attending courses. Its claims about attention spans, learning styles, memory chunks and tutor efficacy were not independently established in this review and do not determine F01's design. Agent task complexity is set by the learning objective and available verified tools. Whether a teaching intervention improves agent performance must be measured independently.
